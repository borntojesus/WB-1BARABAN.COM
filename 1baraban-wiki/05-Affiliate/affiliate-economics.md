## 1. Commission Models — Industry Benchmarks

### RevShare (% of Net Gaming Revenue)

| Vertical                 | Standard range | Premium / negotiated          | Notes                              |
| ------------------------ | -------------- | ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Online casino            | 25–45%         | 50–60% (volume); 70–80% rare  | Most common 30–40% in 2026         |
| Slots (subset of casino) | 25–40%         | up to 50%                     | Often bundled with casino deals    |
| Sports betting           | 25–45%         | 55% top-tier                  | Volatile due to player win streaks |
| Poker                    | 15–35%         | up to 50% (volume affiliates) | PokerStars: 10–35% by RMP count    |

### CPA (€ per FTD)

| Vertical          | Tier-1 EU (UK, DE, NL, IE, SE)      | Tier-2 EU (ES, IT, PL) | Tier-3 / global English |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------- | ---------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Online casino     | €150–€400                           | €80–€200               | €35–€120                |
| Sports betting    | €150–€400 (premium up to €750)      | €100–€200              | €50–€150                |
| Poker             | €100–€200                           | €65–€150               | €50–€100                |
| Crypto / offshore | €50–€250 (often hybrid; looser geo) |                        |                         |

A typical hybrid example: **€65 CPA + 20% RevShare**.

### Hybrid mechanics

- Combine upfront CPA (e.g. €100) with recurring RevShare (e.g. 20–25%).
- The CPA is typically lower than a pure-CPA deal in exchange for the RevShare tail.
- Negotiated _per affiliate_ — not a published rate. Requires existing volume or a credible plan.
- Best for sites with proven traffic quality that need partial cashflow.

## 2. LTV Math (Per-FTD Economics)

### Assumed player profile (Tier-1 EU casino, mid-tier brand)

| Variable                   | Assumption                                 | Rationale                   |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------- |
| Avg deposit/player/month   | €120                                       | Casual recreational players |
| House take rate            | ~5% of stakes; ~30–40% of deposits → NGR   | Slots-heavy mix             |
| Player lifetime            | 4–6 months (heavy attrition after month 2) | Industry retention data     |
| **NGR per FTD (12-month)** | **€280–€420 — midpoint €350**              |                             |

### Per-FTD revenue scenarios

| Deal structure                           | Month-1 | 12-month | Notes                         |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------- | -------- | ----------------------------- |
| Pure CPA €200                            | €200    | €200     | Cashflow-friendly, capped     |
| Pure RevShare 35% (NGR €350)             | ~€30    | ~€122    | Slow build, compounds         |
| Hybrid €65 CPA + 20% RevShare            | ~€85    | ~€135    | Best of both for proven sites |
| Pure RevShare 40% on high-LTV (NGR €600) | ~€50    | ~€240    | Home run, rare                |

### Click economics

| Metric               | Conservative | Realistic | Strong |
| -------------------- | ------------ | --------- | ------ |
| Click → registration | 1.0%         | 2.0%      | 3.5%   |
| Registration → FTD   | 20%          | 30%       | 45%    |
| Click → FTD          | 0.20%        | 0.60%     | 1.6%   |

At 0.6% click→FTD and €200 CPA → **EPC ≈ €1.20**.
At 0.6% click→FTD and €122 RevShare-12mo → **EPC ≈ €0.73 over 12 months**.

These are _good_ numbers in iGaming. Many affiliate sites operate at €0.30–€0.80 EPC blended.

## 3. Cookie Windows, Payment Terms, Minimum Payouts

### Cookie window

- **Standard:** 30 days. Players usually deposit within days of clicking.
- **Common in EU:** 30–60 days.
- **Premium negotiated:** 90 days or session-based.
- **Lifetime cookies:** rare; almost never offered to new affiliates.

### Payment terms

| Term      | Meaning                                    | When you'll see it                  |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- |
| Net-30    | Paid 30 days after end of conversion month | Standard for established affiliates |
| Net-60    | Paid 60 days after end of conversion month | Common for new / low-volume         |
| Net-15    | Rare, premium                              | Negotiated only after track record  |
| Bi-weekly | Some networks                              | Higher trust required               |

Hold periods exist for refund/chargeback protection. Plan for **at least 45 days from first FTD to first payout in cash**.

### Minimum payouts

| Method            | Typical minimum          |
| ----------------- | ------------------------ |
| Bank wire (EU)    | €100–€500                |
| Skrill / Neteller | €50–€100                 |
| Crypto (USDT)     | €50–€100 (sometimes €20) |
| PayPal            | €50–€100                 |

### Cashflow implication

A €200 FTD in March is typically paid late April or May, _if_ the affiliate hits the minimum threshold. Plan for **60–75 days from launch to first cash in bank**. Q1 must be funded from founder runway.

## 5. Recommendation: Deal Structure for a New Site

**Default to CPA in Q1. Move to Hybrid in Q2/Q3. Shift majority to RevShare/Hybrid by year 2.**

### Why CPA-first

- **Cashflow.** A new site cannot fund 6 months of operations on a slow-building RevShare tail. CPA pays in the next payout cycle.
- **Predictability.** Easier to forecast and compare across operators.
- **Negotiation leverage.** CPA is the entry-level offer; networks rarely give RevShare to brand-new affiliates without volume.
- **Validation.** CPA forces clarity on conversion math early — we'll know within 60 days which operators / pages convert.

### Why not pure CPA forever

- **Player-LTV mismatch.** If our chat concierge sends genuinely high-LTV players (the bet), pure CPA leaves 50–80% of revenue on the table over 12 months.
- **Operator relationship damage.** Pure-CPA affiliates with low-quality traffic get shut down. Hybrid aligns incentives long-term.
- **Compounding.** RevShare is the closest thing to recurring revenue a content site has. Year 2+ should look like a SaaS MRR ramp.

### Confidence: ~75%

The CPA-first call is well-supported by cashflow logic and matches what most successful new affiliate sites have done. The risk is if 1baraban's traffic quality is _exceptional_ from day one (because chat filters intent better) — in which case we'd want RevShare immediately. We won't know until we have 90 days of conversion data tied to operator-supplied LTV signals. Revisit at the Q2 review.

## 7. Red Flags to Avoid

- Programs that demand exclusivity at any tier we're at.
- Programs with **negative carryover on RevShare** (your bonus losses roll over to next month — _avoid_).
- Programs combining very long hold periods (>60 days) AND high minimum payouts (>€500).
- Operators with public complaint volumes on AskGamblers / Casino.Guru — we lose user trust by association.
- "CPA-only, no RevShare option ever" from operators with strong brands — they're optimizing against us.
